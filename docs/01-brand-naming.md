## Final direction: TAAB

- One syllable, four letters, no spelling ambiguity; it survives being said out loud in a TikTok.
- The meaning (radiance, lustre) is the promise of every category we sell, without naming any one of them.
- It is Urdu-rooted, so it feels ours, yet it reads naturally in English and Arabic markets.
- Typographically strong: an all-caps wordmark with wide tracking works on a lipstick bullet, a shipping box and an Instagram avatar.
- Tagline: **Radiance, made simple.**
