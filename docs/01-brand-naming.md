# Step 1 · Brand naming

## Final name: Naz & CO

Set by the owner on 4 October 2026. The store started under the working name TAAB (beauty only), became "Naaz & CO" on 3 October when more departments were added, and the spelling was then shortened to **Naz**.

| | |
|---|---|
| Written as | **Naz & CO**. In addresses and handles: `nazandco`. |
| Pronunciation | NAAZ and co |
| Meaning | *Naz* (ناز) is Urdu for pride, grace and the pleasure of being cherished. "& CO" makes it a house rather than a single product line. |
| Tagline | **Good things for you and your home.** |

### Why it works

- **Not tied to one category.** It reads like a department store, so cosmetics, an appliance and a shirt can all sit under it, and new departments can be added without renaming anything.
- **Warm and personal.** It sounds like a family shop someone stands behind, which matters when customers are asked to pay an advance.
- **Easy to say and spell** in Urdu and English.
- **Works with the logo.** The N monogram stands alone as an icon, and the wordmark carries the full name. The wordmark on the site was edited from the supplied artwork ("Naaz&CO") to the new spelling.

Pick a domain from the free ones above (or `nazandco.pk` if PKNIC shows it free), register it, then set `VITE_SITE_URL` in Netlify.

Still to be verified by the owner before anything is printed or advertised:

- Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube handles (`@nazandco` or a variant). None has been checked or created.
- "Naz" is a common name and word, and the matching .com is already taken, so other businesses with this name exist. Do a trademark search at IPO Pakistan (ipo.gov.pk) in class 3 (cosmetics), class 7/9/11 (appliances and electronics), class 25 (clothing) and class 35 (retail) before spending on packaging.

Nothing above claims that a handle or trademark is free. "No registration found" was true for the domains listed at the time of the check and can change at any moment.
