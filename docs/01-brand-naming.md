# Step 1 · Brand naming

## Final name: Naaz & CO

Chosen by the owner on 3 October 2026, replacing the working name TAAB. The store also widened at the same time, from beauty only to three departments: beauty, electronics and kitchen.

| | |
|---|---|
| Written as | **Naaz & CO** (as on the logo). In addresses and handles: `naazandco`. |
| Pronunciation | NAAZ and co |
| Meaning | *Naaz* (ناز) is Urdu for pride, grace and the pleasure of being cherished. "& CO" makes it a house rather than a single product line. |
| Tagline | **Good things for you and your home.** |

### Why it works

- **Not tied to one category.** "Taab" (radiance) only made sense for beauty. "Naaz & CO" reads like a department store, so cosmetics, a power bank and a frying pan can all sit under it.
- **Warm and personal.** It sounds like a family shop someone stands behind, which suits cash-on-delivery customers who buy on trust.
- **Easy to say and spell** in Urdu and English; survives being read out in a TikTok or a voice note.
- **Works with the logo.** The N monogram stands alone as an icon, and the wordmark carries the full name.

Naaz was candidate 16 in the original twenty-name shortlist (kept in the repository history), so the pronunciation, meaning and personality notes from that exercise still apply.

Still to be verified by the owner before anything is printed or advertised:

- Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube handle `@naazandco` (the site links to these as placeholders; none has been checked or created).
- "Naaz" is a common name and word, so other businesses called Naaz almost certainly exist. Do a trademark search at IPO Pakistan (ipo.gov.pk) in class 3 (cosmetics), class 9 (electronics), class 21 (kitchenware) and class 35 (retail) before spending on packaging.

Nothing above claims that a handle or trademark is free. "No registration found" was true for the domains listed at the time of the check and can change at any moment.
